# TriSecure Solutions — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Project Overview
Create a premium, modern, and highly professional corporate website for **TriSecure Solutions**. The website establishes TriSecure as a trusted business solutions partner offering services across:
1. **HR Services**
2. **Insurance & Loans**
3. **Food Compliance**
4. **Digital Marketing**

The website must feel human-designed, intentional, and brand-specific. It should not look AI-generated, template-based, generic, repetitive, or like “AI slop.”

## 2. Primary Goals
- Build strong trust and credibility.
- Clearly communicate TriSecure's complete service ecosystem.
- Make it easy for visitors to understand and select a service.
- Generate high-quality enquiries and consultation leads.
- Present the company as experienced, reliable, and client-focused.
- Create a memorable, editorial visual identity instead of another generic corporate website.

## 3. Website Structure & Navigation
- **Top Navigation**: Minimal transparent/frosted bar with Brand Wordmark, Service Categories, Locations, Why Us, and "Get Consultation" CTA.
- **Hero Section**:
  - High-impact editorial headline and clear value proposition.
  - Primary CTA: "Get Consultation"
  - Secondary CTA: "Explore Services"
  - Subtle interactive status/coverage indicator.
- **Business Introduction**:
  - Comprehensive explanation of TriSecure's integrated enterprise & business solutions.
- **Interactive Service Ecosystem**:
  - Four Core Verticals:
    - **HR Services**: Recruitment & Staffing, Payroll Management, Employee Training & Development, Compliance & Regulatory Affairs, Employee Relations.
    - **Insurance & Loans**: Personal Loans, Home Loans, Business Loans, Auto Loans, Insurance Solutions.
    - **Food Compliance**: FSSAI License, Shop & Establishment, Health & Trade License, DPCC License, Application assistance, Compliance support, Renewals.
    - **Digital Marketing**: Strategic brand positioning, performance marketing, search & digital growth.
- **Featured Solutions & Interactive Checklists**:
  - Deep-dive interactive views for each vertical (eligibility, compliance readiness calculator, timeline guide).
- **Why TriSecure**:
  - Expertise & Regulatory Insight
  - End-to-End Comprehensive Services
  - Client-Centric Execution
  - Reliability & Long-term Support
- **How We Help (4-Step Workflow)**:
  - Understand → Recommend → Execute → Support
- **Location / Service Discovery Search Component**:
  - Searchable by City / District / State for service availability & field support.
- **Trust & Compliance Assurance**:
  - Highlighting strict procedural adherence, fast turnarounds, and ongoing advisory.
- **Lead Generation / Consultation**:
  - Clean, high-converting consultation form with immediate feedback and clear confirmation states.
- **Footer**:
  - Corporate identity, vertical directory, location footprint, contact info, and regulatory disclaimers.

## 4. Design & Aesthetic Rules (Matching DESIGN.md)
- **Palette**: Walnut Shadow (`#100904`), Warm Cream (`#ffedd7`), Bark Brown (`#382416`), Cork Border (`#40372e`), Driftwood (`#6c5f51`), Ember Accent (`#dc5000`).
- **Typography**: Halyard / Inter with strict dual voice:
  - Uppercase weight 500 for headers, nav, labels, tags, and buttons.
  - Mixed-case weight 400 at 29px / 18px for body descriptions.
- **Motion**: Subtle, purposeful Framer Motion transitions (scroll reveals, tab shifts, modal transitions). No bouncing, floating clutter, or generic AI blobs.
- **Icons**: Strictly purposeful functional icons only.

## 5. Technical Requirements
- React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
- Tailwind CSS v4 + Design Tokens CSS Variables
- Framer Motion for smooth transitions
- Lucide React for clean, minimalist functional UI icons
- Fully responsive (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop)
- Accessible, SEO optimized, performant (<100ms TTI)
